Simulation 9

Coach Education

Student Simulation Summary

Health care providers (HCPs) will schedule a meeting with a coach to discuss a medical condition for an athlete. There are three possible medical conditions to discuss: sickle cell trait, asthma, and epilepsy. Students will be informed within 24 hours of the meeting which condition they will be addressing. Students should prepare for all three.

Simulation 13

Shoulder Injury

Student Simulation Summary

The purpose of this simulation is for health care providers (HCPs) to demonstrate the ability to evaluate an acute upper-extremity injury and apply immediate first aid and management for other possible injuries.

These activities are designed to enhance your learning experience and help you succeed during the simulation. Your instructor may assign specific or all activities. We encourage you to read each of the supplemental readings and be prepared to be tested on them.

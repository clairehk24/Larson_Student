Simulation 11

Modalities Shuffle: Cold and Hot

Student Simulation Summary

Health care providers (HCPs) will complete three stations with a brief case at each station, requiring the HCP to decide on a superficial hot or cold modality, compression, or manual biofeedback to treat the injury. Students (HCPs) will then set up the modality, answer patient/proctor questions, and then move to the next scheduled location.

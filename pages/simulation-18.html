Simulation 18

Cervical Spine Injury

Student Simulation Summary

This situation will occur in a county ice arena. The health care providers (HCP) are visiting, per diem athletic trainers (AT) for an amateur ice-skating competition. The competition is for ages 16 and up. Today is practice, and the competition is tomorrow and the following day. During a practice, one athlete was dropped on their head.

Introduction

Welcome to the world of simulation education! You are likely reading this because your instructor has expressed interest in recreating some of the more common or life-threatening conditions health care providers (HCPs) in a sport setting encounter. You might be wondering, “What is simulation?” Simulation is the replication or creation of an experience or situation for the purpose of providing an experience, practicing, or assessing skills. Your instructor has chosen at least one simulation from the Clinical Simulations for the Athletic Trainer in which you will participate.

The goal of the simulation is to provide you the opportunity to experience, demonstrate, or learn the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to be a successful clinician. Most importantly, the simulation experience is designed to give you confidence in your abilities as a clinician. Please speak to your instructor about any questions or concerns you have. The instructor will explain the simulation that was chosen and provide you with information on expectations. We encourage you to enjoy the adventure!

Be sure to follow the instructions found at the beginning of this ebook for accessing the HKPropel resources! Each of the simulations includes activities that your instructor may assign for you to complete before or after the simulation. On HKPropel, you will find the activities as downloadable documents that you can download, complete, and upload to your instructor. Your instructor may also use HKPropel to deliver simulation quizzes, so be sure to access HKPropel!

In addition, enhance your learning and clinical confidence with the free Athletic Training Clinical Resource—available exclusively to purchasers of this textbook. This curated resource brings together essential information used in health care, including normal lab values, vital sign norms, ICD10 and CPT medical codes, and approved medical abbreviations. Whether you’re reviewing chapters, preparing for exams, or working with patients, this tool kit keeps the foundational information you need right at your fingertips. See the inside front cover for a QR code to access this free resource, or visit Ancillaries.HumanKinetics.com/AthleticTrainingClinicalResource.

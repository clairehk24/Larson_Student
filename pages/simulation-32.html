Simulation 32

It All Comes Together at the Hip

Student Simulation Summary

The purpose of this simulation is for health care providers (HCPs) to demonstrate the ability to evaluate and treat a chronic hip injury.

Presimulation Activities

These activities are designed to enhance your learning experience and help you succeed during the simulation. Your instructor may assign specific or all activities. Please speak to your instructor with any questions. Presimulation Activity 1: Supplemental Readings is in the ebook. We encourage you to read each of the supplemental readings and be prepared to be tested on them.

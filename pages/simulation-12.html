Simulation 12

Modalities Shuffle: Electrotherapy and Ultrasound

Student Simulation Summary

Health care providers (HCPs) will be assigned a modality case in advance of the simulation. This advance preparation will allow one student to participate as a patient, a second to serve as a proctor, and the third to participate as the HCP. Each HCP will complete three stations with a brief case at each station, requiring the HCP to decide on and use a modality to treat the injury. The HCP will answer the patient’s and the proctor’s questions before moving to the next scheduled station.

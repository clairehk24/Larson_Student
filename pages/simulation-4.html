Simulation 4

Special Test Roulette—Lower Extremity

Student Simulation Summary

Students will research and serve as a patient and proctor of a specific injury requiring a special test. As a health care provided (HCP), students will participate at 5 stations by completing special tests and answering patient or proctor questions at each station.
